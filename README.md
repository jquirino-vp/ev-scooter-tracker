## What's Included in v2.2.0

### GPS Speedometer, Odometer & Ride Mode
- Smooth 1 Hz damped GPS speedometer with live compass heading, incline/slope (%), current elevation, and elevation gain/loss
- Zero-scroll landscape and portrait Handlebar Ride Mode with compact lower-right live map and 1-tap Hide Map mode for offline riding
- Resettable Trip A and Trip B sub-odometers alongside your lifetime GPS odometer
- Configurable Auto-Pause and Auto-Resume on stops
- EV audio chimes (startup, lower standby ready chime until moving, shutdown, pause/resume, and 6-second milestone chimes at 30, 40, 50+ km/h) with speedometer flash and haptics

### Multi-Chemistry Battery Monitor & Charging Advisor
- Supports Lead-Acid (EV Deep-Cycle 6-DZF SLA, AGM, Gel, Flooded), Lithium-Ion (NMC/NCA), and LiFePO4 packs with Observed Full Resting Voltage calibration
- Recalibrated two-stage CC Bulk + CV Absorption charging time estimates
- Live and historical Estimated Remaining Range (unlocks after 2+ rides with voltage data)
- Electricity cost tracker (total, 30-day, per-km, per-charge), equivalent charge-cycle counter, and V/km baseline health trend
- Odometer-based scooter maintenance reminders

### Trips, Tags & Exports
- Ride tags (Commute, Errand, Long Ride, Passenger, Hills, Rain) with tag-filtered V/km comparison
- Export rides as GPX 1.1 (with elevation), CSV, full JSON backup/restore, or shareable 1080x1350 PNG ride summary cards
- 100% local on-device storage with no account or cloud server required

## How to Install on Android
1. Download the `.apk` file from **Assets** below.
2. Open the downloaded file on your Android phone.
3. If prompted, enable **Install unknown apps** for your browser or file manager.
4. Tap **Install** and grant **Location (GPS)** permission when starting your first trip.

## Important Notes
- GPS speed, elevation, range, and battery SOC are estimates for reference only.
- Always operate controls while parked and keep your eyes on the road.
